Delete Your Account

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This page explains how to delete your Sawa account and what happens to your data. Sawa ("Sawa", "we", "us") operates the Sawa mobile application (the "App"), a marketplace that connects entrepreneurs seeking funding with investors in Lebanon.

Delete your account from within the App

You can delete your account at any time, directly in the App:

  1. Open the Sawa app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm when prompted. Your account and data are then permanently removed.

Request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the App, you can request deletion by emailing sawaappadmin@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We will verify your identity and process the request.

What data is deleted

When you delete your account, we permanently remove:

  • Your profile information (name, email, phone number, profile photo).
  • All listings you created, including their photos, financial details, milestones, use-of-funds, and projections.
  • Your saved listings.
  • Your profile-view records and verification records.
  • Your chat messages and conversations.

What data is kept

We do not retain personal data after deletion, except where we are required to keep limited records to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or prevent fraud and abuse. Any such records are kept only for as long as the law requires and are then deleted. Automated backups are purged on a rolling basis, typically within 30 days.

Questions about account deletion or your data? Contact us at sawaappadmin@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
