Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Sawa ("Sawa", "we", "us") operates the Sawa mobile application (the "App"), a marketplace that connects entrepreneurs seeking funding with investors in Lebanon. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account information you provide when you register: your name, email address, phone number, password (stored encrypted), and an optional profile photo.

Listing content you choose to post: business name, description, category, location, photos, financial details, and any documents you upload.

Messages you exchange with other users through the in-app chat.

Location data (optional): if you grant permission, we use your device location to show listings near you. The App works fully if you decline, and we do not track your location in the background.

Device & technical data: a push-notification token and basic device information needed to deliver notifications and operate the App.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and show your profile and listings.
  • To let you browse listings, save them, and message other users.
  • To show listings near you (only if you enable location).
  • To send you notifications related to your activity (you can turn these off).
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

3. How Information Is Shared

With other users: information you add to a public listing or your public profile (name, role, business details, photos) is visible to other users of the App. Messages are shared with the user you are chatting with.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Service providers: we share data only with providers that help us run the App (see "Third-Party Services" below), under their respective terms.

Legal: we may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

Sawa only facilitates introductions. No payments, investments, or financial transactions occur inside the App, and we are not a party to any agreement between users.

4. Third-Party Services

The App relies on trusted providers to function: Google Firebase (authentication, file storage, and push notifications), MongoDB Atlas (database hosting), and cloud server hosting. These providers process data on our behalf. If you sign in with Google, your use of that service is also subject to Google's privacy policy.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secured cloud servers and databases. Passwords are stored in encrypted (hashed) form, and data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. While no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

6. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your personal information and your listings from our systems, except where we must retain certain data to comply with legal obligations.

7. Your Rights & Choices

  • Access & update: you can view and edit your profile and listings in the App at any time.
  • Delete your account: you can delete your account and associated data from within the App (Profile → Delete Account).
  • Location: you can allow or deny location permission in your device settings.
  • Notifications: you can turn off push notifications in your device settings.

8. Children

Sawa is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the App or by other appropriate means. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at fred@sawaapp.co.
